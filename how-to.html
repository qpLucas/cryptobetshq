How to get started

Everything you need to know about depositing crypto and placing your first bet on Cloudbet.

1. Create your Cloudbet account

Sign up takes under 2 minutes — just an email and password. No phone number or KYC required at signup.

Create account ↗
2. Deposit cryptocurrency

Go to your Cloudbet wallet, choose your coin (BTC, ETH, USDT etc.), copy your deposit address and send from your wallet. Deposits confirm in 1 blockchain confirmation.

3. Follow our tips

Check our daily tips on the homepage. Click any tip to see the full analysis, pick and odds. All picks link directly to Cloudbet where you can place the bet.

4. Withdraw your winnings

Withdrawals on Cloudbet are processed instantly to your crypto wallet. No withdrawal limits for verified accounts.
